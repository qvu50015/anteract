Product is a UCI exclusive social connection app designed to help students meet new friends, activity partners, study partners, and potential romantic partners in a safer and more familiar environment. Users would create an account using their UCI email to verify that they are currently attending the university.

##
Reference art:
Visual direction combines UCI’s blue-and-gold identity with a modern and friendly social-app aesthetic. The reference art includes campus locations such as Aldrich Park, ARC(sth. like that), along with rounded profile cards, interest tags, playful illustrations, and subtle anteater-inspired elements. The overall design should feel welcoming, energetic, safe, and recognizable to UCI students
